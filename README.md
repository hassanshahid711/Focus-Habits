No account or install is needed. Your data is saved on your own device and is private to you.

Quick start (2 minutes):

Habits tab: tap Quick add routines, pick a category and tap Add on a few habits (start with 2 or 3, not 10).
Focus tab: choose a subject, set your energy, write what you’ll finish, then tap Start focus.
After each session, rate it honestly (Yes / Partly / No).
Each day, tap ✓ for the full habit or ½ for the tiny version. The tiny version still keeps your streak alive.
Review tab: check it once a week for your summary and one suggested change.

Handy features:

Tiny version: on a bad day, do the minimum and keep the streak.
Auto-check: habits can tick themselves after your focus sessions.
Themes: change the look from the menu at the top right.
Collapse sections: tap any section title to hide or show it.
Guide tab: full instructions for everything.

Good to know:

Data is stored in your browser, so clearing browser data or switching phones starts you fresh.
On your phone, use Add to Home Screen in your browser menu to open it like an app.
